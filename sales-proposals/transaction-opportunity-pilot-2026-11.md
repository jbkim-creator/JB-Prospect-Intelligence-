# JB Prospect Intelligence — Transaction Opportunity Sprint (2026-11-10)

## Positioning
Public-evidence research for genuine B2B purchase, supply and disposal opportunities. Not a promise of confirmed orders, signed mandates, or revenue.

## Proposed commercial package (test hypothesis, not approved tariff)
- Qualified Opportunity Sprint: **KRW 990,000 + applicable VAT** for one product category, one target geography and 10 business days, subject to separate scope agreement.
- Deliverables: up to 10 sourced opportunity records, including public source URL, event/lot ID, company, item, specification, quantity if available, known deadlines, official contact route, verification flag, priority, and recommended next action.
- Minimum validation: publicly evidenced opportunity, or explicitly marked 'demand unverified'; no unsubstantiated claim of purchase interest.
- Replacement of invalid/duplicate records: within 5 business days of customer feedback; hard-to-verify demand must be replaced or marked unresolved and excluded from accepted count. Define source cut-off before engagement.
- No direct outreach, named-introduction, negotiation, confidential data transfer, or exclusive representation without separate written consent and fee/legal review.
- Payment suggestion: 50% on signed scope and 50% on acceptance; discretionary until signed agreement.
- Existing Pilot 30: retain existing contractual terms for any ongoing conversation, do not silently convert or reprice.

## Qualification Gate
1. Buyer/seller identity + geography; 2. actual item, full PN and spec; 3. quantity and deadline; 4. budget/asking price if known; 5. source URL and evidence date; 6. contact route and ownership authority; 7. duplicate check; 8. next action; 9. source/recipient disclosure authorization; 10. fee eligibility.

## Initial target segments
Industrial equipment OEMs with export sales, components distributors, asset-recovery/surplus operators. Favor inbound conversations first. Do not re-solicit NET (contacted Nov 9), Lulin (contacted Nov 10), or Markwin (deferral response Nov 9) before a meaningful new milestone.

## Weekly revenue gate
Qualified inquiry → proposal → signed paid scope → first invoice paid → accepted report → renewal or separately agreed transaction support. Record actual deposit, not notional deal value.

## Example data columns
opportunity_id,client,category,country,item_or_PN,spec,qty,deadline,price_range,source_url,source_date,source_type,contact_route,verification_status,duplicate_key,probability_basis,fee_contract_status,stage,next_action,next_action_date,invoice_amount,received_amount

## Red lines
No guessed buyers, scraped personal contact data, fake RFQs, or forwarded private inventories. No unlicensed securities / real-estate / investment solicitation. Preserve opt-out and suppression lists.
