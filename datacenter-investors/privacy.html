Investor inquiry privacy notice

Jbro Capital Ltd. uses the contact and investment mandate details you provide through this website to review institutional investment interests, respond to your inquiry and consider relevant transaction introductions.

Information collected

Institution name, contact name, work email, country, investor category, expected investment range, target geography, strategy and optional mandate comments.

Processing and hosting

Submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms. Hosting and processing may occur outside your country. Access should be restricted to authorized operating personnel. Please do not submit sensitive personal identification or confidential financial records.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for inquiry handling and relevant investor-network engagement, subject to applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction or deletion, email jbkim@jbrocap.com. Data may be shared with service providers operating the website; investment profiles are not publicly posted.

No investment offer

Registration does not create an advisory relationship, obligate the company to introduce any transaction, or constitute an offering of securities.
