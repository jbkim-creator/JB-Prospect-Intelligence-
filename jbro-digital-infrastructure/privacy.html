Privacy Notice — Contact Enquiries

Draft for pre-launch legal review. Updated 8 November 2026.

Jbro Capital Ltd. (Seoul, Republic of Korea) processes information submitted through the website enquiry form to respond to your business enquiry and manage related correspondence.

Information collected

Name, work email, organisation, enquiry category and your message. Please do not submit confidential offering documents, personal financial information or sensitive personal data through this form.

Purpose and basis

Business enquiry handling and reasonable follow-up, based on the consent provided with the form. We do not treat form submissions as investment orders, financing mandates or commitments.

Service providers

Website hosting and form processing may be performed by Netlify or the selected hosting provider. Information may be processed outside Korea. Final provider details, overseas-transfer disclosures and required safeguards must be reviewed before the form is activated.

Retention

Proposed retention: up to 12 months after the last substantive enquiry communication, unless earlier deletion is requested or law requires longer retention. Confirm this policy before launch.

Your rights

For access, correction or deletion requests contact jbkim@jbrocap.com. Where applicable, you may withdraw consent.

Website analytics

This pilot site does not intentionally include analytics or advertising tracking scripts. If these are added, this notice and consent workflow must be updated.

This preliminary notice is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review.
