Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee orders or distributor appointments?

No. We provide scoped market research and approved B2B outreach. Commercial outcomes depend on product fit, price, regulation and customer decisions.

Who signs purchase agreements and handles payments?

Typically the manufacturer and Korean buyer contract and settle directly. We do not hold inventory or customer funds under the pilot service.

When do you disclose target buyer identities?

Buyer identity disclosure, registered-account protection and transaction commissions should be agreed in writing before introductions when applicable.

Which sectors do you cover?

Initially industrial pumps, valves, CNC/automation components and data-center infrastructure components. Regulatory certifications and existing channel exclusivity require separate checks.
