## Mandatory transaction gates (all must be verified)
1. **Identity & authority** — full legal names, jurisdictions, beneficiary bank ownership, signing authority, sanctions and counterparty due diligence as applicable.
2. **Stock & authenticity** — actual warehouse evidence, title to goods/authorized sale, batch/DC, manufacturer and provenance, testing, counterfeit controls; reject untraceable high-risk lots if no adequate risk mitigation.
3. **Commercial alignment** — exact manufacturer part, quantity, price currency, quote expiry, shipping, import taxes, quality acceptance, returns, delivery address, no undisclosed party fee.
4. **Intermediary economics** — separate executed written referral/success-fee agreement with payer(s), method of calculation, payout event, cancellation/refund rules and reasonable noncircumvention restrictions; include FIRST10K eligibility/waiver explicitly if applicable.
5. **Purchase agreement** — only actual seller/buyer sign and pay one another; JBRO remains intermediary unless a separate, intentional principal/trading arrangement is executed. No intermediary impersonation or payment custody.
6. **Platform access** — create consented and verified partner applications; approve, issue each party its own restricted access; open Deal Room only when its mandatory verified fields and terms are available. Do not use fake contact data or import emails as user consent.
