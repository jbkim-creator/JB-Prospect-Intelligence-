Participation principles

We review each inquiry. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee a partnership, transaction or payment. Confidential information sharing, deal access and success fees require separate express authorization and written agreements. Approved inquiries are stored for partner screening only when the intake service confirms successful submission. If intake is unavailable, no online submission occurs and a manual email option is provided.
