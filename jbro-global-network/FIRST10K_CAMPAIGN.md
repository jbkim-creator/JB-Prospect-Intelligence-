- Track funnel: page visits, requests, qualified applications, coupons actually issued, signed trades, commission receipts, and total fee concessions. Starting pilot cap per coupon US$250. Establish an aggregate marketing budget before scaled promotion.
- The internal database contains legacy field names such as `discount_won` and `deal_value_won`; they store **integer minor units** for USD records. Always inspect the accompanying `currency=USD` value and present USD cents properly in all new APIs and screens. Legacy KRW records retain their existing denomination.
