## Exclusions & integrity
- Only **one specified 2.5% JBRO fee stream** is eligible per new approved fee-paying buyer OR seller, not both sides.
- Must be applied **before** fee-sharing agreement issuance; no retroactive adjustment of existing agreements.
- No split transactions to collect multiple benefits; no automatic eligibility upon submission; a valid authorized buyer or supplier and lawful transaction are required.
- Waiver affects **actually collectible brokerage fee**, and therefore any success-fee participant rewards must be computed from actual fee cash received after discount.
- Any third-party expense, VAT, freight, insurance, inspection charges or separately negotiated non-JBRO fees remain outside the waiver.
