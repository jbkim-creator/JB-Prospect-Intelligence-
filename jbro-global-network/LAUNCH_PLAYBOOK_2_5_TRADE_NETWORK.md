# 2.5 Trade Network by JBRO — Launch Playbook

**Status: provisional marketing display brand; trademark/domain clearance pending.** Do not claim registered, licensed, trademark-cleared, escrow-backed, or guaranteed trade outcomes.

## Positioning
- Official display brand: **2.5 Trade Network by JBRO** in both Korean and English.
- English tagline: **Global Deals. Shared Success.**
- Korean: **글로벌 거래기회를 발견하고, 팀을 구성해, 실제 거래를 완성하세요.**
- Audience: first-wave verified semiconductor IC/MLCC/memory buyers and stockholding suppliers, followed by industrial surplus and sourcing contributors.
- Core experience: **Discover → Quest → Team Up → Deal Room → Contracted Rewards**. XP is nonfinancial; contract-backed monetary rewards require real bank-confirmed JBRO commission and settlement approval.
- Mission access requires invitation and JBRO qualification. Application is not a transaction, guaranteed commission, or customer-verified supply.

## FIRST10K campaign
- Eligible first-time approved buyer OR supplier on ONE eligible **USD** deal, only its specified agreed JBRO 2.5% fee stream.
- First **US$10,000 of trade principal** has the applicable JBRO fee waived; maximum **US$250** waived. Amount above threshold charged at agreed 2.5% rate, subject to signed terms. Buyer and seller streams are not both automatically discounted.
- Not stackable, retroactive, or automatically issued. Taxes, FX costs and third-party services excluded. KRW deals not automatically converted.

- Landing URL Korean: https://jbro-global-network.onrender.com/?lang=ko#first-trade-offer
- Quest URL English: https://jbro-global-network.onrender.com/?lang=en#experience
- Quest URL Korean: https://jbro-global-network.onrender.com/?lang=ko#experience

## First 30 days (targets, not achieved results)
- Week 1: verify the bilingual campaign page, partner account flow, contact consent and intake reference, create a deduped spreadsheet of 30 IC/MLCC buyers and 30 suppliers, prepare existing-thread followups.
- Week 2: tailored outreach to 20 previously uncontacted verified organizations and follow up only when appropriate in existing threads. Avoid bulk unsolicited campaigns. No claims of stock availability without a live verification.
- Week 3: 4 EN and 3 KO trade-education posts, publish anonymized process examples only; no customer logo without permission.
- Week 4: aim for 5 qualified applications, 2 independently verified RFQ/inventory matches and 1 fee-agreement-backed deal room. These are pilot **targets**, not reported results.

## CRM metrics
- Unique inbound inquiries by country/role/source (where legally disclosed), coupon interest vs actual coupon issuance, approved active partners, qualified RFQs, independently verified stock offers, matches, contract acceptance, bank-confirmed commission, coupon discount, net JBRO receipt, approved contribution rewards, repeat trade.
- A URL with a campaign parameter is **not** proof of attribution until the backend stores the approved source. Do not claim conversion tracking is live without testing it.
